# SUBSET-023 v4.0.0: Glossary of Terms and Abbreviations

ERA, CCS TSI Appendix A, index 003. The terms and abbreviations of the
ERTMS/ETCS specifications: what SUBSET-026 means by a word it does not
define in place (balise group location reference, active cab, location
based information, conditional level transition order). The terms are a
two-column table in `sections/04_terms.md`, the abbreviations in
`sections/05_abbreviations.md`; `grep -n -i '^|TERM' sections/04_terms.md`
finds one.

## What the on-board takes from it

A reading of a SUBSET-026 clause that turns on a term's meaning cites the
glossary entry beside the clause (in the plan's decision and the matrix
note); the glossary defines no behaviour and has no rows in the matrix.

The PDF is the reference; `sections/` holds its text as markdown
for searching and reading, generated by
[../tools/import_subset.py](../tools/import_subset.py). Tables that
span pages are split, formulas and figures are lost or garbled: check the
PDF page (`<!-- end of page N -->` comments) before relying on a detail.
A clause starts its own line: `grep -rn '^3.1.1.1 ' sections/`.
