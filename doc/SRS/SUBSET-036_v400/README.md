# SUBSET-036 v4.0.0: FFFIS for Eurobalise

ERA, CCS TSI Appendix A, index 009. The Eurobalise transmission system: the
air gap, the up-link balise, the on-board transmission equipment, and the
coding of telegrams. Most of the text is unnumbered paragraphs under
numbered headings, so search by heading or by words rather than by clause.
The PDF is the reference; `sections/` holds its text as markdown for
searching and reading, generated by
[../tools/import_subset.py](../tools/import_subset.py). Tables that span
pages are split, formulas and figures are lost or garbled: check the PDF
page (`<!-- end of page N -->` comments) before relying on a detail.

## What the on-board takes from it

The BTM port ([EVC-PLAN.md §1](../../EVC-PLAN.md)) delivers decoded
telegrams: the user bit string of SUBSET-026 chapter 8.4.2, after the
1023/341 bit decoding of this document (telegram format and coding in
4.3), which stays outside the EVC. The air gap and the on-board
transmission equipment are out of scope; the timing and distance
requirements of a balise passage (4.2.9) are what SUBSET-041 5.2.1.9 builds
on.
