# SUBSET-040 v4.0.0: Dimensioning and Engineering Rules

ERA, CCS TSI Appendix A, index 013. The engineering rules the trackside
follows and the on-board may rely on: the distances between balises in a
group and between groups, the antenna's place on the engine, the
dimensioning of balise group announcements, the rules for older system
versions (appendix). Each rule is a table (Rule, Reference,
Justification) under a numbered heading in `sections/04_rules.md`.

## What the on-board takes from it

The constants the on-board and the bench of `sim/` and `test/src/` choose
where SUBSET-026 gives none (the length of a balise group a passage waits
for, the spacing of the bench's balises, the antenna offset of the
configuration) are read from its rules and cite them; a SUBSET-076
sequence whose track breaks a rule is triaged with the rule named. No
rows in the matrix: the rules bind the trackside.

The PDF is the reference; `sections/` holds its text as markdown
for searching and reading, generated by
[../tools/import_subset.py](../tools/import_subset.py). Tables that
span pages are split, formulas and figures are lost or garbled: check the
PDF page (`<!-- end of page N -->` comments) before relying on a detail.
A clause starts its own line: `grep -rn '^3.1.1.1 ' sections/`.
