# **11. CONFIGURABILITY**

## **11.1 Description**

11.1.1.1 The ATO-OB shall be independent of trackside configuration and shall operate on updated data received from the ATO-TS in the form of updated versions of JPs and SPs.

11.1.1.2 Line extensions and modifications of trackside layout shall be configurable in the ATOTS.
