# SUBSET-125 v1.1.0: ERTMS/ATO System Requirements Specification

ERA, CCS TSI Appendix A, index 084. The requirements of the ATO on-board
and trackside: journey and segment profiles, the driving functions, the ATO
states and their transitions, the DMI information of ATO, the interfaces to
the ATO trackside, the ETCS on-board and the rolling stock. The PDF is the
reference; `sections/` holds its text as markdown for searching and reading,
generated by [../tools/import_subset.py](../tools/import_subset.py). The
clause numbers of this PDF are images: the tool read 600 of them back with
OCR and checked each against the sequence (the PDF has no outline to check
them against); three list labels on page 41 are not restored. Tables that
span pages are split, figures are lost: check the PDF page
(`<!-- end of page N -->` comments) before relying on a detail. A clause
starts its own line: `grep -rn '^10.2.2.8 ' sections/`.

## What the on-board takes from it

The ETCS side of Automatic Driving ([EVC-PLAN.md §1](../../EVC-PLAN.md)):
the ATO on-board is outside the EVC, a separate module on the ATO port
(packets in [SUBSET-130](../SUBSET-130_v100/README.md)); the EVC implements
the ETCS-side conditions of SUBSET-026 4.4.16 and 3.15.11. From this
document it needs the ATO-OB / ETCS-OB interface of 10.2 (communication
management and its time-outs, the data the ETCS-OB sends, driver inputs),
the ATO engagement conditions (9.1.2) and the ETCS mode transition request
(9.12), and the DMI information of ATO in chapter 8, which the DMI already
displays.
